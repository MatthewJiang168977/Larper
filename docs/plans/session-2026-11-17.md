### Backup and event retention correction

Backup objects become eligible for lifecycle cleanup at 60 days of object age. Actual
removal depends on when cleanup runs. The roughly three-week estimate assumes successful
weekly base backups and a 14-day recovery window. Restores may bring back deleted data.
Broker retention applies to closed event segments and also depends on background cleanup.
These settings replace the earlier snapshot's 60-day purge guarantee.
